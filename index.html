{{ t.jIntro }}

GEAR-DRIVEN FIRE CONTROL

Two gearboxes. Exact, repeatable heat.

Two gearbox sets drive the grate height with cast-iron handles — exact, repeatable positions over the coal bed. No electronics, nothing to fail, the same position every service.

MATERIALS & CONSTRUCTION

Stainless steel 304, welded to last.

A 1.5 mm stainless pipe structure with fully welded joints resists thermal deformation; a 9 mm stainless charcoal tray takes the heat. The front panel is finished in high-temperature black coating, rated to 600 °C.

ASH MANAGEMENT

Clear the ash, mid-service.

An integrated charcoal support and ash drawer let you clear ash quickly and hygienically without stopping — clean airflow, consistent combustion, and less cleaning time at the end of the night.

QUALITY & DURABILITY

More than a grill. A cooking system.

Built to shape a barbecue culture that lasts — heavy, refined and finished to a standard you keep, and cook on, for a lifetime.

BASED ON CRAFT — MATERIALS & MAKING

One master. One grill.

PREMIUM MATERIALS

Bodies in stainless steel 304 from POSCO and Hyundai steel — corrosion-resistant, uncommonly thick and built to last. Material certificates available on request.

ONE MAN, ONE GRILL

Each grill is entrusted to a single master — from the first cut of steel to the final inspection. One person's responsibility in every weld, not a hundred hands on a line.

BUILT TO MOVE, BUILT TO LAST

A heavy-duty lower frame on casters keeps a 120 kg tabletop moving with the line, while 304 throughout meets professional hygiene standards.
